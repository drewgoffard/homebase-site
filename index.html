Homebase

A family calendar and household hub for a touchscreen on your wall or counter.

Homebase runs on a small computer (a Raspberry Pi) inside your home. It shows your family's calendars, lists and weather on one screen, and can add events to Google Calendar from the wall.

  • No account, no cloud service and no subscription.
  • Your family's information stays on the device in your home.
  • Open source under the GNU AGPL.
